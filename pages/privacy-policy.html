Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

Dark Skull Corporation is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our website, Android applications, software products, and related services.

Effective Date: August 2026

Overview

We believe privacy should be simple and transparent. We collect only the information necessary to operate our services, improve our applications, maintain security, and provide a better user experience.

Information We Collect

Depending on the service you use, we may collect:

  • Device model and operating system
  • Application crash reports
  • Anonymous usage statistics
  • Information submitted through contact forms
  • Account information (where applicable)

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information unless required for a specific feature.

How We Use Information

Your information helps us:

  • Improve application performance
  • Fix bugs and crashes
  • Maintain security
  • Respond to support requests
  • Develop new features
  • Enhance overall user experience

We never sell or rent your personal information.

Android App Permissions

Some applications may request permissions only when required for a specific feature.

  • Camera — QR code scanning
  • Storage — Save generated files
  • Notifications — Reminders and alerts

Permissions are always requested only when necessary.

Third-Party Services

Our applications may use trusted third-party services including:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase (future products)
  • Google AdMob (supported apps only)
  • Analytics services (if enabled)

These services operate under their own privacy policies.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember preferences
  • Improve website performance
  • Provide a better browsing experience

You may disable cookies through your browser settings.

Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Our products are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without parental consent.

If such information is discovered, it will be removed as quickly as reasonably possible.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access your information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Contact us regarding privacy concerns

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect improvements, legal requirements, or new services.

The latest version will always be available on this page with the updated effective date.

Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
